Profile

Professional Summary

I work as a Research Engineer in Post Quantum Cryptography at the KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence, University of Cyprus, with a research focus on the security of PQC-enabled communication protocols and AI-driven cybersecurity.

My expertise spans artificial intelligence, embedded systems, and security engineering. I design ML/RL-powered solutions for vulnerability detection, automated testing, and advanced fuzzing, while performing functional, robustness, and penetration testing on embedded devices. My work focuses on securing smart-grid infrastructures across DLMS/COSEM, HDLC/M-Bus, and IEC 60870-5-104 protocols, supported by scalable cloud pipelines using Linux, Docker, and AWS.

I hold an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Cyprus, completed on a full scholarship. Earlier, I worked as a Research Engineer at the KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence, as an Embedded Security Tester at ENCS, and as an Electrical Engineer at Huawei Technologies.
